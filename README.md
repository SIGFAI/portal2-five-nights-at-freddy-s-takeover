# Five Nights at Freddy's Takeover

**Portal 2** × Five Nights at Freddy's · visual · idea by the team

Bring Five Nights at Freddy's into the game in the most visible and funny way. The change shows in the first 30 seconds of play.

> I turned a Portal 2 chamber into Five Nights at Aperture: turret animatronics creep in whenever you look away. Foxy screamed at me mid-portal.

## Install

Put the mod folder (scripts, sound, materials) together with the kit's scripts folder into one Portal 2 mod folder. Start Portal 2 with -game and that folder plus +sv_cheats 1, load a test chamber and survive until 6 AM with your flashlight.
